## Selection policy

1. Prefer the official Korean storefront or an official hosted Korean storefront with KRW pricing.
2. Otherwise prefer an official `en-kr`, Korean country/currency selector, or official English storefront.
3. Otherwise use the brand's official home-country site.
4. Do not substitute an unrelated retailer merely to eliminate a null.
5. Verify identity against both the official brand/about page and products already attached to the brand node when the name is ambiguous.

Each updated row also records `homepage_verified_at`, `homepage_sources`,
`homepage_storefront`, optional `homepage_currency`, and a verification note in
`brand_nodes.wiki`. The exact source URLs and the guarded replay script are in
`tools/update-brand-homepages-5793-5867.ts`.
